# v0.2.2 — v0.2 hardening

v0.2.2 closes the post-release trust-boundary and crash-consistency review of the complete v0.2 line.

- **Outbound privacy and consent.** Local-input-derived child diagnostics cannot enter repair requests. Retained-history repair now previews the exact bounded receipt subset and requires affirmative terminal approval. Telemetry completion, feedback, and parent acknowledgement recheck opt-out and the current notice while serialized by the send lock.
- **Bounded program lifecycle.** The wall deadline now covers descendant-held output pipes and cleanup, lingering process trees are classified as timeout, and workspace accounting rejects symlinks instead of traversing outside the private workspace.
- **Durable recovery.** Resume reconciles a crash after a managed rename but before item-state persistence, forced-restore provenance stays sticky, prune dry-runs are read-only, retained snapshot bytes are enforced globally, and stale preparation can age out safely.
- **History integrity.** Full retained intent is truly byte-bounded, truncated intent cannot seed repair or recovery, redacted exports use an allowlist without IDs, hashes, or stale checksums, failed attempts preceding repair remain in the timeline, and history clearing preserves separately consented recovery evidence.
- **CLI truthfulness.** Management operands preserve argv boundaries such as paths containing spaces; repair feedback and recovery guidance no longer require an undocumented separator; shell edits re-enter validation; `context show` uses the resolved request shell/context path; unsupported parent-shell fallbacks fail explicitly.
- **Release assurance.** Package inclusion is root-anchored, installation creates the destination and verifies checksums portably, release publication depends on the complete CI workflow, and every release runner executes the native behavioral suite before packaging.

The v0.2.1 proxy, doctor-exit, parse-time usage error, local-notice, version-text, replay-notice, and recovery-pinning fixes remain pinned by regression coverage.
