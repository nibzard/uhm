# Code of conduct

`uhm` should be a place where people can disagree about code without making the
work personal.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and respectful.
- Critique the change, not the contributor.
- Assume mistakes are ordinary and fixable.
- Give newcomers enough context to act on feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not accepted in project
spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems through a private maintainer or platform moderation
channel when possible. Do not force someone to discuss harassment in a public
issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior.
Good-faith disagreement about technical choices is not a conduct violation.
